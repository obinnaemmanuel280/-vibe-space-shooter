Hold the vibe. Clear the sector.

Arrows to choose, Enter to select

Pilot name

Enter a username to play. It appears on the leaderboard.

How to Play

Move

Arrow keys or WASD. On touch, drag anywhere.

Shoot

Your jet auto-fires at the nearest enemy. Space or FIRE shoots straight ahead.

Pause

P or Esc. Restart with R.

R

Rapid fire: four shots, faster.

S

Shield: blocks hits for a few seconds.

H

Health: restores 25 hull. Hull also regenerates slowly when unhit, and each cleared wave repairs 12.

B

Bonus: +500 points.

Survive 10 levels (3 waves each) to win. Each level promotes your pilot rank, from Cadet to Ace. Big asteroids split. Gunners shoot back and drop bombs. Press X or tap INTERCEPT to launch 10 homing missiles at bombs and enemies (10 second reload). A Warlord arrives every fifth wave. You have 3 lives.
